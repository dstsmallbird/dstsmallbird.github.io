About Me

Greetings!

I’m currently an associate professor at School of Information Science and Engineering, Lanzhou University. With over fifteen years of practice, my research has covered a wide range of areas, including penetration testing, code analysis, malicious sample generation and detection, and security management. Specifically, my recent work has focused on open-source security and AI-synthesized speech governance.

I am the founder and director of N05ec Team. My team is from the community and work for the community. We have gotten more than 200 CVEs from the community. Currently, I’m the core organizer of the Lanzhou Google Developer Group (GDG) and the deputy secretary general of the OpenAtom OpenHarmony Security Committee. I also serve on the editorial board or program committee in several journals and conferences. Please feel free to contact me if you have any novel ideas or cooperation plans on exciting security topics.
